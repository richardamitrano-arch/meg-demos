Studio Kit: purchase and privacy

This page covers the data involved when you buy Studio Kit Pro and use the kit.

Who is responsible

MEG Web Studio, Via Cantonale 34a, 6928 Manno, Switzerland, info@megwebstudio.com.

When you buy

  • Checkout and payment run on Shopify, under Shopify's own privacy policy. We do not see your card details.
  • The download email and the download link also come from Shopify: after payment its digital-delivery app sends the email, hosts the file and counts the downloads of each order, to apply the download limit.
  • We see your order in our Shopify admin, with what you entered at checkout: name, email address and billing address, plus the order number, items and amount.
  • Shopify also sends each paid order of our shop to our own order system, which keeps a copy. An order it cannot match to a website project within 24 hours triggers an alert to us on Telegram, with the order number, amount and email address.
  • The zip is the same for every buyer: nothing about you is written into it.

Why

To deliver your download, send a new link when you ask, switch off the link after a refund, and check that a copy is licensed. Legal basis: performance of the contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)); Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.

Where and for how long

Orders, the file and the download email are on Shopify. Our own order system runs on Railway (hosting). Both handle the data on our behalf. The alerts of the order system reach us through Telegram. Records are kept for 10 years, the retention period of Swiss accounting law, then deleted.

The kit itself

The kit adds no telemetry. Its skills are text files and its scripts run on your machine; launch-qa and visual-qa load only the URL you give them. Claude Code, which runs the kit, sends your prompts and the context it reads to the model under Anthropic's terms.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to info@megwebstudio.com. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the data protection authority of your EU country.
