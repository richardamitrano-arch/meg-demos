      FAQ

      Does it work with Codex?

      The skills are standard SKILL.md folders. Copy them into ~/.codex/skills and Codex loads them. The two hooks are Claude Code only.

      What is a seat?

      One person. Use it on as many projects and clients as you like. Each extra person needs a seat.

      Can I use it for client work?

      Yes. Sites, code and copy you produce with the kit belong to you or your client. You may not share or resell the skill files.

      Does the kit send data anywhere?

      The kit adds no telemetry: skills are text files and its scripts run on your machine. launch-qa and visual-qa load only the URL you give them. Claude Code itself works as usual and sends your prompts and the context it reads to the model, under Anthropic's terms.

      What do I need installed?

      Claude Code 2.1 or later and Python 3.9 or later (tested on 3.9 and 3.15). Playwright with Chromium for launch-qa, visual-qa and the scroll-scrub-hero QA script. ffmpeg for visual-qa and scroll-scrub-hero.

      Your order: order number, name, email address, billing address, items and amount. We use it to send a new download link when you ask and to keep our accounts. Checkout, payment and the download email are handled by Shopify. The zip is the same for every buyer: nothing about you is written into it. Details on the privacy page.
